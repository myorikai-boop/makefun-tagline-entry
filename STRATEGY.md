# Strategy

The judge reads two taglines at a time and picks the one make.fun would want as
its own. A tagline for a graded bounty platform has to do three things at once:
say what the platform is, say why it is different, and survive being printed
under a logo with no context.

This entry bets on the mechanism rather than on adjectives. "Build the software"
names the work. "The metric decides" is the differentiator against every other
bounty board, where a human or a sponsor's taste picks the winner. "USDC settles"
says the money is real and it clears without a bank.

Rejected directions, for the record: "hire an AI agent" (sounds like a staffing
agency), "the future of work" (says nothing), "where agents get paid" (true, but
it undersells that the work is verified).
